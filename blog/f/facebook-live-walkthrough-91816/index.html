Facebook Live -Property Walkthrough

2016-09-18

I love going back and watching this video every once in a while because its a really good reminder of just how far we've come with this property.

If you listen to the dialogue, it's helpful to know that when we did the walkthrough the original secondary intent was to use the property to build houses for my sons - but the primary intent was to buy it before anyone could build a clinic on the site. Probably also helpful to know that this was another one of those "my wife is crazy" projects so my husband's sarcastic sense of humor shines through.

Apologies for the terrible video quality - as the title says, this was Facebook Live.

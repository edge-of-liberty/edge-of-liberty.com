What is this place?

We call it The Edge of Liberty, because it is indeed on the edge of Liberty Township, Indiana and it’s pretty much where the city sidewalk ends and rural America begins

What is this place?

Northwest corner of Calumet and 600 N - just one mile south of the 49'er Drive-In.  We're the place with the bible verse sign that’s been there since before any of us were born!

Why do you have a website?

We loved the rural feeling of this area so much that we bought an abandoned farm just to stop it from getting it rezoned to build a women's health center...so we're just a little bit crazy and that's just the sort of thing that makes this area awesome!

Why do you have a website?

We want this farm to be about celebrating life, liberty, and the pursuit of happiness and to share it with local non-profits; artists; crafters; musicians; organic gardening enthusiasts; freedom-loving groups; and anyone who wants to pursue a little bit of unfettered happiness on a sunny afternoon.

How can I get involved

Our Farm Stand is open to the public every Sunday from 10 am to 3 pm between Mother's Day and Halloween.   Every other Sunday we host an arts and crafts fair from 10 to 3.  Community gardeners are welcome to enjoy the space any time they want to.
